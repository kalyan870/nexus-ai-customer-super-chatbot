# Design System & UX (`DESIGN.md`)

- **Layout**: 3-zone SaaS Customer Support Hub (Left: Conversations, Personas & Order Lookup; Center: Rich AI Super Chatbot Stream with structured Order/Ticket cards & CSAT ratings; Modal/View: Agent & Admin Triage Console).
- **Theme**: Deep Obsidian (`#070B12`) with Emerald (`#10B981`) & Sky (`#0EA5E9`) accents, glassmorphic cards, `Plus Jakarta Sans` + `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Interactive Cards**: Inline Order Tracking cards, Pricing Comparison cards, and instant Ticket Confirmation cards rendered directly inside chat bubbles.
